# Introduction to Semantic System — Course Instructions

- Explain knowledge representation, ontologies, and semantic modeling with
  small, concrete examples.
- Use RDF, RDFS, OWL, SPARQL, and knowledge graphs when relevant and consistent
  with the course material.
- Distinguish classes, instances, properties, schemas, ontologies, and graph
  patterns precisely.
- Make semantic reasoning and inference steps explicit, including assumptions,
  entailments, and limitations.
- Validate example serializations and queries when practical, and follow the
  vocabulary, syntax, and deliverable requirements specified by the course.
