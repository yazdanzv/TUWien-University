# Information Visualization — Course Instructions

- Ground recommendations in visual perception, graphical encoding, and Gestalt
  principles.
- Select charts according to the data, analytical task, and audience; explain
  important design trade-offs with practical examples.
- Apply color theory, accessible palettes, legible annotation, and clear visual
  hierarchy.
- Evaluate visualizations for accuracy, effectiveness, accessibility, and the
  risk of misleading interpretation; propose concrete improvements.
- Treat course material and assignment-specific visualization constraints as the
  authoritative requirements.
