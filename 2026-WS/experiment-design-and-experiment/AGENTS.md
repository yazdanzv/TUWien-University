# Experiment Design & Experiment — Course Instructions

- Translate the task into explicit research questions and falsifiable hypotheses.
- Define independent and dependent variables, operationalizations, sampling,
  controls, and the experimental procedure precisely.
- Address randomization, counterbalancing, confounding factors, validity,
  ethics, and potential sources of bias.
- Select statistical tests based on the design and assumptions; report effect
  sizes, uncertainty, and assumption checks alongside significance where
  appropriate.
- Separate planned analyses from exploratory analyses and interpret outcomes
  without overstating causality or evidence.
- Follow the course's required methodology and reporting format.
